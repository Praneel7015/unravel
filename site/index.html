CREATOR’S MANIFESTO · § 04

Why Unravel Exists

Most mental wellness apps are engineered like slot machines. They wrap your quietest vulnerabilities in day-streak counters, flame icons, push-notification guilt, and unencrypted cloud databases so they can report “Daily Active Users” to venture capital boards.

Anxiety cannot be healed by an app that makes you anxious about losing a streak. Unravel was built as an antidote: a tactile field journal that steps out of the way. You open it only when an upcoming moment feels knotted in your chest, untangle what belongs to your craft from what belongs to fortune, and return after the moment has passed to see—in your own data—that reality is almost always gentler than fear predicted.
